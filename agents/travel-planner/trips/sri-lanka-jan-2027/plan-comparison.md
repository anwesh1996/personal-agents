## Gaps in their plan (fill from ours / research)

1. **Arrival flight must be morning Sat or Fri night** — evening Sat arrival breaks Day 9 Colombo schedule.
2. **Colombo hotel early check-in** — request when booking; Fri-night booking is cleanest.
3. **Nine Arch at 11:30 after leaving NE at 9:00** is late/crowded — leave earlier or do Nine Arch at sunrise on Ella morning (needs 2N Ella).
4. **Kandy afternoon stack** (palace + Tooth + Bahirawakanda after 1pm arrival) is tight — start Tooth first; palace if open.
5. **Temple dress code** + SAARC ticket machines.
6. **Whale trip** — book licensed boat; 6:00 harbour; seasickness tablets; not guaranteed.
7. **Grand Hotel** — confirm room sleeps 3; January rates often ~USD 150–250+ / night (indicative).
8. **Casino** — passport ID; modest dress; set a cash loss limit (not in trip budget).
9. **Thai Pongal Fri 15 Jan** — south coast busier.
10. **Driver quote must include** fuel, tolls, parking, driver room/meals, late casino return, airport run.
11. **Return:** Galle leave ~14:00 only safe for **16 Jan ~21:50** flight; for **17 Jan early** need Negombo night 16th.
12. **Vegetarian** — no Maldive fish / shrimp paste.
13. **Optional train** Nanu Oya→Ella while driver relocates with bags.
